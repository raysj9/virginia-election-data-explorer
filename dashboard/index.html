How to compare results

The percentage-point change compares Democratic two-party share in the same congressional district. It describes party results across two elections; it does not identify how individual voters behaved. District boundaries, candidates, and election conditions can change. Candidate votes are reported choices, not a turnout measure.

Data and AI usage

With an API key, your question, the last 12 conversation messages, and this saved public election data are sent to OpenAI. API usage may incur charges. Without a key, answers use limited local lookups.
